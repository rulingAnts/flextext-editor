# Corpus Keeper — moved

The Corpus Keeper plan, its issues and its code live in their own repository:
https://github.com/rulingAnts/corpus-keeper (plan: `plans/corpus-keeper.md`; Parallels
port-forwarding guide for the phase-2 bridge: `docs/parallels-port-forwarding.md`).

What stays in this repo is the suite's side of that plan: the consent-per-person change, the
Speaker field, `media-file` and `speaker` attributes in the timed FLExText, morpheme tiers in the
ELAN export, and the panel's Assign-from-Outbox / Receive-to-Inbox entries (tracked as issues here
when work starts), plus #49, the FLEx-backed audio segmenter, which uses the keeper library as its
sidecar.
