    Buttons on the coworker's Texts screen

    These travel with your setup/task links and decide which "new text" buttons the coworker sees on their Texts screen.

    Speaker permission before recording

    This reminder appears on the coworker's device before each new recording. A recorded "yes" is saved alongside the text (in the zip), never inside the transcription audio.

    Use this when you set up a coworker's own phone in person. To bring the Settings tab back: tap the ? (Help) button 7 times (or press Ctrl+Alt+R on a keyboard).

    Task link (text + audio)

    Optional: send an already-transcribed (or fully glossed) FLEx text for checking/editing. The app reads its writing-system codes and will not build a link unless they match your vern/anal setup above.

    Phone Recording link

    Audio sent through WhatsApp is recompressed and loses quality, even inside a zip. Prefer Upload to Google Drive.

    Recording format

    Off by default — recordings capture the raw microphone level. Turn this on to let a too-quiet or too-loud device be adjusted.
