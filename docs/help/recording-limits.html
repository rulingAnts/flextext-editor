Recording limits on real devices

For researchers setting up a worker's device. It covers what a cheap phone can and cannot do, what the app does when it reaches a limit, and which settings to choose when a device is too limited for archive-quality recording.

The short version. A recording is never lost because a device ran out of room. The app stops the recording first, keeps it, and offers it for review as normal. What you lose is length, not the take. If workers are regularly hitting the limit, the fix is a different recording format — see When a device is too limited.

Why lossless recording has a length limit

The lossless formats (WAV and FLAC) are held in the phone's memory while recording, and only written out when the worker saves. Longer recording means more memory. If a device ran out completely, the browser would close the page and the recording would be gone with no warning and no way to get it back — so the app stops before that point instead.

The compressed formats (Opus and MP3) do not work this way. They are written out continuously while recording, so they have no memory limit at all. That difference is the single most useful thing to know on this page.

What the app actually does

There are two separate limits, and whichever comes first wins:

  1. The maximum length you set for that worker, if you set one.
  2. A memory limit the app works out from the device itself. You do not set this and cannot turn it off — it is the safety net.

As a recording approaches the memory limit, the worker sees "About 3 min left on this device" on the recording screen, plus a one-time message asking them to finish soon. If they keep going, the recording stops by itself and goes straight to the review screen with the recording intact, and says so plainly: nothing was lost, listen to it and save it.

What actually ends up in the saved file

  • The file is made fresh from the captured audio when the worker saves. The clip they hear on the review screen is a throwaway preview at lower quality; it never becomes the saved file, and it never affects it.
  • Recordings start a few seconds early. The app keeps the last few seconds of sound from before the Record button was pressed, because speakers often begin before the button. Expect up to about four seconds of room noise at the start of every file. This is deliberate — better a little room noise than a missing first word.
  • If the device cannot do the format you chose, the app records in the closest one it can and says which. It never silently gives you something different.
  • The file that uploads is the file that was saved — nothing is re-compressed on the way out.

What a phone genuinely cannot do

Some archive requirements cannot be met by a phone browser, no matter which settings you choose. It is better to know this than to collect files that look compliant and are not.

"24-bit" from a phone is not the same as 24-bit from a recorder

Bit depth is a property of the file, not the microphone. A phone's audio hardware resolves roughly 16 bits of real detail. Recording at 24-bit gives you headroom — protection against distortion — but not extra detail. The file is honestly 24-bit; the microphone was never that good. Do not describe such a file to an archive as though it came from a 24-bit recorder.

A web browser cannot capture a chosen bit depth at all

By the design of the web audio standard, a browser always captures in a 32-bit floating-point form and the app converts afterwards. The conversion is faithful, but "the worker chose 24-bit and the device truly recorded 24-bit" is only provable in the Android app, which records through the phone's audio system directly.

Automatic gain

The app leaves automatic gain off by default, which is what archives require. There is an auto setting that switches it on in Chrome-based browsers, because Chrome drives the microphone level high and gain control is the only protection a browser has against distortion. Recordings made with automatic gain on are not archival masters — the loudness has been altered while recording. Use it only when the alternative is a distorted, unusable recording.

Wireless microphones are never archive quality

A Bluetooth headset compresses and processes the audio before the app ever receives it, so the result is not a master no matter which bit depth is selected. The Android app reports which microphone was actually used and marks such recordings accordingly. Use the phone's built-in microphone, or a plug-in one.

When a device is too limited — what to change

In order of how much they help, and how much they cost you:

1. Record mono, not stereo (free)

Doubles the available length and loses nothing — one person talking into a phone is a mono source.

2. Use 16-bit WAV instead of 24-bit (nearly free)

Half the memory, so double the length, and on a phone microphone you give up almost nothing real — the extra depth was headroom rather than detail. 16-bit WAV is uncompressed and accepted by most archives. This is usually the best first change on a limited device.

3. Set a maximum length yourself

If you set it below the device's own limit, workers get a predictable stopping point instead of discovering the limit mid-sentence. Consider splitting long texts into named sections — often better for the work anyway.

4. Switch to Opus (WebM) — no length limit, but not an archival master

Opus removes the length limit entirely and produces small files that upload easily on a poor connection. The cost is real and permanent: it is lossy, so detail is discarded and cannot be recovered. Suitable for transcription, analysis, and getting the work done. Not suitable as a preservation master.

Be honest about the trade. A lossy recording that exists is worth far more than an archival recording that was never made because the device could not manage it. But do not later describe those files to an archive as masters. If a text matters enough to preserve, record it again on a better device — or record it on a device you control and import it.

5. Use the Android app instead of the browser

For a device that keeps hitting limits and where the recordings genuinely matter, the Android app records straight to a file. It has no memory limit, can record a true bit depth, can switch off the phone's audio processing, and reports which microphone was used.

Batas perekaman pada perangkat nyata

Untuk peneliti yang menyiapkan perangkat pekerja. Menjelaskan apa yang bisa dan tidak bisa dilakukan ponsel murah, apa yang terjadi saat mencapai batas, dan pengaturan mana yang dipilih bila perangkat terlalu terbatas untuk rekaman kualitas arsip.

Ringkasnya. Rekaman tidak pernah hilang karena perangkat kehabisan memori. Aplikasi menghentikan rekaman lebih dulu, menyimpannya, dan menampilkannya untuk ditinjau seperti biasa. Yang hilang adalah durasi, bukan rekamannya. Jika pekerja sering mencapai batas, solusinya adalah format rekaman yang berbeda.

Mengapa rekaman lossless punya batas durasi

Format lossless (WAV dan FLAC) disimpan di memori ponsel selama merekam, dan baru ditulis saat pekerja menyimpan. Makin panjang rekaman, makin besar memorinya. Jika perangkat benar-benar kehabisan memori, peramban akan menutup halaman dan rekaman hilang tanpa peringatan dan tidak bisa dikembalikan — jadi aplikasi berhenti sebelum titik itu.

Format terkompresi (Opus dan MP3) tidak bekerja begitu. Keduanya ditulis terus-menerus selama merekam, jadi sama sekali tidak punya batas memori. Perbedaan itu adalah hal paling berguna di halaman ini.

Apa yang dilakukan aplikasi

Ada dua batas terpisah, dan yang lebih dulu tercapai yang berlaku: durasi maksimum yang Anda tetapkan, dan batas memori yang dihitung aplikasi dari perangkat itu sendiri (tidak bisa Anda atur atau matikan — ini jaring pengaman).

Mendekati batas memori, pekerja melihat "Sekitar 3 menit lagi di perangkat ini" di layar rekaman, disertai satu pesan agar segera menyelesaikan. Jika diteruskan, rekaman berhenti sendiri dan langsung ke layar tinjauan dengan rekaman utuh, dan menyatakan dengan jelas: tidak ada yang hilang, dengarkan lalu simpan.

Apa yang sebenarnya tersimpan di berkas

  • Berkas dibuat ulang dari audio asli saat pekerja menyimpan. Klip yang terdengar di layar tinjauan hanya pratinjau berkualitas lebih rendah; itu tidak pernah menjadi berkas yang disimpan.
  • Rekaman dimulai beberapa detik lebih awal. Aplikasi menyimpan beberapa detik terakhir sebelum tombol Rekam ditekan, karena penutur sering mulai bicara lebih dulu. Harap maklum ada sekitar empat detik suara ruangan di awal setiap berkas — ini disengaja.
  • Jika perangkat tidak mendukung format pilihan Anda, aplikasi merekam dengan format terdekat dan menyebutkan yang mana. Tidak pernah diam-diam menggantinya.
  • Berkas yang diunggah adalah berkas yang disimpan — tidak dikompresi ulang.

Yang benar-benar tidak bisa dilakukan ponsel

"24-bit" dari ponsel tidak sama dengan 24-bit dari perekam profesional. Kedalaman bit adalah sifat berkas, bukan mikrofon. Perangkat keras ponsel hanya menghasilkan sekitar 16 bit detail nyata. Merekam pada 24-bit memberi ruang aman terhadap distorsi, bukan detail tambahan.

Peramban tidak bisa merekam pada kedalaman bit pilihan sama sekali. Sesuai standar audio web, peramban selalu merekam dalam bentuk 32-bit floating-point lalu aplikasi mengonversinya. Hanya aplikasi Android yang bisa membuktikan bahwa perangkat benar-benar merekam pada kedalaman yang dipilih.

Penguatan otomatis (AGC) mati secara bawaan, sesuai syarat arsip. Pengaturan auto menyalakannya di peramban berbasis Chrome. Rekaman dengan penguatan otomatis bukan master arsip — kenyaringannya sudah diubah saat merekam.

Mikrofon nirkabel tidak pernah berkualitas arsip. Headset Bluetooth mengompresi dan memproses audio sebelum aplikasi menerimanya. Gunakan mikrofon bawaan ponsel atau mikrofon berkabel.

Bila perangkat terlalu terbatas

  1. Rekam mono, bukan stereo. Menggandakan durasi, tanpa kehilangan apa pun.
  2. Gunakan WAV 16-bit, bukan 24-bit. Setengah memori, durasi dua kali lipat, dan pada mikrofon ponsel hampir tidak ada yang hilang. Biasanya perubahan pertama yang terbaik.
  3. Tetapkan durasi maksimum sendiri, di bawah batas perangkat, agar pekerja punya titik berhenti yang bisa diperkirakan. Pertimbangkan membagi teks panjang menjadi beberapa bagian.
  4. Beralih ke Opus (WebM). Menghilangkan batas durasi dan menghasilkan berkas kecil yang mudah diunggah. Biayanya nyata dan permanen: lossy, detail dibuang dan tidak bisa dikembalikan. Cocok untuk transkripsi dan analisis; tidak cocok sebagai master pelestarian.
  5. Gunakan aplikasi Android. Merekam langsung ke berkas: tanpa batas memori, kedalaman bit sungguhan, pemrosesan audio ponsel bisa dimatikan, dan melaporkan mikrofon mana yang dipakai.

Jujurlah tentang pertukarannya. Rekaman lossy yang ada jauh lebih berharga daripada rekaman arsip yang tidak pernah dibuat karena perangkat tidak sanggup. Tetapi jangan kemudian menyebut berkas itu sebagai master kepada arsip.
